The receipt slips

This is about the slip the desk printer makes every half hour from your phone's notifications. It decides what prints right away, what waits for the slip, what goes at the top, and what never prints at all.

Eight decisions, in order. Each one has a suggestion marked; change anything in the notes box. Nothing changes in Home Assistant until your marks are in. The box at the bottom always shows your marks as plain text, so they can't get lost.
