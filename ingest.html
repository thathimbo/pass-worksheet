Getting things in

One front door, one tidy-up step, then Deck. Every source (Echo, phone, email, the desk) drops raw words in the same inbox. A curator tidies them and leaves a suggestion. The card lands in Deck's Backlog with the To review label, and The Pass ignores it until you take the label off. The curator never picks your next task, never moves a card to To Do or Doing, and never sets priority.

Five decisions. Pick what fits, change anything in the note box. The box at the bottom always shows your marks as plain text, so they can't get lost.
